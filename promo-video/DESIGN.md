# WRC Product Introduction

## Style Prompt
An energetic, human-centered running-club introduction. Use the WRC brand's deep forest green and vivid lime, real club imagery, generous breathing room, clear Chinese type, and steady forward motion. The product should feel welcoming and practical, with people and shared progress at the center.

## Colors
- Deep forest green: `#0B503B` — primary canvas
- Vivid lime: `#D9FF3F` — emphasis and action
- Warm white: `#F4F5EF` — primary type
- Muted sage: `#B8C8BE` — secondary type
- Dark green: `#07372B` — depth and UI panels

## Typography
- `Hiragino Sans GB` for Chinese display and body copy
- `Arial` for Latin labels and compact numeric readouts

## Motion
Smooth, confident entrances and short crossfades. Keep ambient motion restrained and let the words and runner imagery carry the energy.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not show personal data as though it were real.
- Do not invent product features or promise outcomes.
- Do not use neon gradients, generic blue/purple SaaS styling, or decorative frames around the logo.
- Do not pack the screen with tiny UI text.
